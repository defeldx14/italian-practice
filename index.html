Private Google Drive sync

Sync after saving on your computer, then sync on your phone. Words, edits and review progress travel together.

Not connected. Local saves and study work without sync.

Use the same Google account and Cloud project on both devices. No full-Drive access. Reconnect when authorization expires. Connection requires internet.

Transfer a backup

No sync setup yet? Export on your computer, transfer the JSON to your iPhone using Files, iCloud Drive or email to yourself, then import here.

Import merges entries and review history; it does not replace your library. Backups include source links and deleted-entry history. Store them privately.

Offline & installation

Open this app online once to prepare offline study. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Browser data can be cleared by iOS; keep backups.

New translations require internet. Missing translations stay blank and can be edited. Public app hosting never includes your private word list.
